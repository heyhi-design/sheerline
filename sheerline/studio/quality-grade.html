The read is yours

◆ Yours to fill
What would you check next, given these trends? The series points at intent fidelity climbing and coherence holding flat. Whether that is the artifact getting better or a signature worth keeping is a taste read. This slot ships empty on purpose. The evaluator will not fill it.
◆ what never renders here
No threshold is drawn on any chart, and no pass mark sits under any series. Calibrated thresholds, the evaluator's internals, and the populated corpus are the retained evaluation moat; they never render in this prototype. What you see are simulated shapes, not calibrated scores.
