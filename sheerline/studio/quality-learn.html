The boundary that keeps a taste read from hardening into a rule

Factual and behavioral

Factual ▸ injected at generation time

Deterministic, structural facts. The conformance lane catches them, and they are injected into generation.

Behavioral ◆ reminds a human

Taste reads. Surfaced to a human as a reminder, never auto-injected and never promotable to an enforced rule.

Promotion

How a note becomes a rule

The threshold to promote

5 or more total fires, or the same pattern across 3 or more separate sessions. When either fires, the soft note is no longer doing its job; it becomes a hard rule and the entry is superseded.

What can never promote

A behavioral correction is never promotable. A taste read hardening into an enforced check is the boundary collapsing. Promotion applies to factual patterns only.

◆ the human curates
The registry is curated, not auto-generated. An agent may observe a recurring pattern and propose a correction; a human decides what enters and what promotes. The intake and the live registry stay separate. Unreviewed proposals never reach agent context.
