Every session that produces a document, artifact, plan, or decision record exits through this gate. The handoff cannot be emitted without it. Each item requires a concrete artifact under a named header, not a claim that the work was done. An empty header is a visible failure; advisory prose is not.

THE HUMAN'S OBSERVATION

◆ Yours to fill
The one named observation is the human's read of the rendered artifact. It stays open on purpose (law L4), and the handoff emits with it still open. The agent renders and reports; the human looks and calls it.

The evidence is the agent's, and it is required. The observation is the human's, and it is meant to stay open. An empty header is the failure the gate catches; an open judgment slot is not.
