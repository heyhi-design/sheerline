The quality loop: Retrieve→Validate→Grade→Learn

Quality · the deterministic floor

Validator report

◐ simulated report artifact: sample button set · focus-states audit from the Session (Retrieve → Generate) →

The floor reports structure, not taste. A conformance lane runs deterministic, structural checks over the artifact at zero token cost. It reports what is structurally true or false. It renders no grade and approves nothing.

Everything above the floor (does the focus order read for an assistive-technology user, is this deviation intentional) routes to a human. The floor being clear is not approval. Approval is the human's, on the Grade screen.
