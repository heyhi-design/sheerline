Capability registry

The capability gallery

Every governed capability as a card. What a capability will not do reads as plainly as what it will: the not-for lines sit beside the triggers, because knowing where a capability stops is how the router learns to say no.

heyhi-skills/

Scope is a governance state, not a folder. A capability is governed when the design-practice registry indexes it with triggers, not-for lines, and a load cost. The wider roster is real and deployed; it is simply not the design subset this workspace routes over.

Capabilities in the pipeline are tracked as candidates until they ship. A candidate is a proposal, not a governed capability, so it carries no triggers and the router will not reach it. When one is built and indexed, it moves into Governed.
