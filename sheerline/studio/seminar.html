Seminar

The reading room

A self-paced ladder from novice to subject-matter expert on how keel and Sheerline actually work. Each track is a level; later tracks assume the earlier ones. Every module is one reading unit built from the same eight parts, and every card points at the module in the repo, not a copy of it.

How a module is built

Eight parts, every module. Read the Concept, do the Walkthrough with the files open, then the Lab.

Separability rides on each module's teaches tag. teaches: keel modules teach the general harness and are written to lift cleanly into keel's own public documentation later, without a rewrite. teaches: sheerline modules teach Sheerline, which is Confidential and gated, and stay internal until those gates open. A keel module may never depend on a Sheerline internal to make sense; the smoke test enforces it.
