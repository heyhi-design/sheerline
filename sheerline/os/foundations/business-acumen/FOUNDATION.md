# Foundation: Business Acumen (area 10)

**Coverage: PARTIAL. This foundation is now filled (P3): `kind: stub → governance`. Coverage does not move at the fill.** Area 10 was already `partial` on the strength of one deployed skill, `experiment-readout-linter` (P1.5), so filling the foundation changes only the foundation state. `partial → covered` is a later, registry-state judgment, earned when the two skills this foundation names below promote (spec § 10 D4). The fill authors the **craft canon** the area-10 skills consume and **points at** the always-loaded invariants; it restates none of them. Its distinctive contribution is the **caveat-by-construction**: the design-business canon's most-quoted numbers are correlational and weak-provenance, and this foundation states that once, structurally, so no skill downstream can launder them into a causal claim.

The area's first job is *not being fooled*: by a recycled statistic, by a peeked experiment, by a competitor's polish, by an automated severity score. Business Acumen is an operating area above the craft spine, not in it.

## The knowledge canon (authored here — the skills' reference)

This is the craft the foundation genuinely authors. The caveat-by-construction is the **first row on purpose**.

| Topic | The canon (authored here) |
|---|---|
| **The design business case caveat-by-construction** | The two most-quoted design-ROI figures are **correlational and weak-provenance**, and are routinely misquoted as causal. **(1)** The McKinsey Design Index (*The Business Value of Design*, 2018; ~300 companies, three industries, five years): top-quartile scorers showed revenue growth 32 percentage points higher and total-returns-to-shareholders growth 56 points higher than industry peers. McKinsey's own text calls this a *correlation*. Provenance status: `verified-primary`, `correlational` (traced 2026-09-05). **(2)** The recycled "every $1 invested in UX returns $100, a 9,900% ROI" figure: attributed to a paid Forrester report (circa 2016) that secondary sources cite and never link or quote directly; the figure's method is not open to inspection, and it is frequently conflated with a separate, equally weak-provenance IBM Systems Sciences Institute "100x cost to fix after development" claim. Provenance status: `partially-verified` at best (an attribution exists; the primary is not inspectable), `causal-as-stated` (traced 2026-09-05). **Canon rule:** a business case may cite either figure only inside a labeled caveat block that carries its provenance status, **never as a headline causal claim**, and the caveat block is inserted by construction whenever any design-ROI figure appears. |
| **Competitive analysis in the designer lens** | The mature workflow: scoping → the competitor set (**always including "do nothing,"** per Dunford's positioning framing) → heuristic teardowns with **dated screenshot evidence** → a **living feature/pattern matrix** → the positioning insight → a refresh cadence. A teardown reads *what a competitor's product does to its user*, not *whether a company is a prospect*. (Facts of record: `ai-services/design-intelligence/research/designer-workflows-methodology-research-2026-07-16.md` § 3.3.) |
| **The what-NOT-to-copy discipline** | NN/g's copy-trap warning as a standing rule: a competitor's pattern is evidence of a choice, not evidence the choice was right for that competitor, and never evidence it is right for you. The matrix records presence; the human decides what to copy. A teardown output that recommends copying has exceeded its charter. |
| **The reference-corpus licensing posture** | Mobbin, and its kin Refero and Nicelydone: **query-time reference for reasoning only; nothing persisted; never a screen in a client-facing artifact** (the 2026-06-17 R1 ruling, branch (a) only; the underlying third-party copyright is one the corpus cannot license). The corpora are **null on civic / infrastructure / government UI** (R3), HeyHi's differentiated territory. So teardown evidence is **practitioner-captured from the competitor's own public product**, dated and attributed, and a reference-corpus hit is a pointer (URL plus app name, stamped `retrieved_at` on HeyHi's side), never an image. The connector's authorization state is a runtime observation, checked per session, never assumed. |
| **Data literacy as not being fooled** | Experiment literacy: peeking inflates a nominal 5% false-positive rate materially under continuous looks (the corroborated range is roughly 20–40%; a single false-precision figure is not cited); power and sample sufficiency; sample-ratio mismatch; the back-solved metric. `experiment-readout-linter` operationalizes all of it. **The text-to-SQL reliability ceiling:** frontier text-to-SQL sits roughly at 10–21% on Spider 2.0, and enterprise reliability is worse; analytics answers come through **semantic-layer templates, never free query synthesis**. The free "chat with your data" skill (`guided-funnel-reader`) is a standing recommend-against and stays unbuilt. Analytics answers "what"; it rarely answers "why" (the User Research foundation's analytics-as-research row, pointed at). |
| **Severity honesty** | Automated heuristic evaluation reliably detects **presence**; **severity is the weak joint**. The 2026 measurement puts exact human–automated severity agreement at roughly 56%, with an agreement coefficient near zero (facts of record: `sheerline-skill-buildout-research-2026-08-09.md` § 6.2). Rule: a skill may flag that a heuristic issue is *present*; the severity, the score, and "does it matter" ship empty. The User Research foundation's severity formula (frequency × impact × persistence, advisory-never-gate) is the kin rule, pointed at. |
| **The stakeholder buy-in structure** | Problem → evidence → response → **measurable expectation**. Each step is structured, none is asserted: the evidence carries a provenance status per claim (`verified-primary` / `partially-verified` / `unverifiable` / `unsourced`, plus `correlational` / `causal-as-stated`); the response is the design investment *described*, not chosen; the measurable expectation names candidate signals and hands to `design-measurability-mapper` for the metric scaffold. The business claim itself stays human. **No pricing, ever.** |
| **Design-team ops** | Thin, and said so. HeyHi's own machinery is the worked DesignOps artifact (`os/operating-principles.md` § DesignOps as a governed practice, area 12). No area-10 skill "runs ops." Deepen only on engagement pull. |
