# Worked example — a filled Divergence Read

**Synthetic input, invented product. No client content.** Read this to calibrate the register and depth of the prose slots; do not copy its content. Both ⛔ slots are empty and the gate is unsigned on purpose. Notice what is absent: no score, no pass or fail, no ordering of the stimulus, no instruction to go back to lo-fi.

**Synthetic session brought to the guard:** a folder with three hi-fi comps for the Fieldnote Market guest checkout, a wizard-style flow rendered in a warm palette, a cool palette, and a monochrome treatment, plus a note: *"Test question: are the checkout steps right for a first-time buyer? N = 3 was the target."* No Lo-fi Pack is in the folder.
